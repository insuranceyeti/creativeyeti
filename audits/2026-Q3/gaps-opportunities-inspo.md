## Problem-mirroring lanes left open

This is the cross-cutting read that only exists when the paid field and the organic field are held together, and for this brand it inverts the usual finding.

**The usual shape does not apply.** Normally the whole field runs solution-oriented creative and nobody mirrors the customer's problem until she sees herself in it. Here, the category's organic field mirrors the problem beautifully and does almost nothing else. The two highest-conversation videos in the 23-video library are pure laments with no solution attached, at comment rates of **1.79%, roughly twelve times the library's typical rate**. `verified` upstream. @molly_daw's entire eighteen seconds is *"Hey, how are y'all affording kids?"* and it drew 557 replies from 31,200 views. Nobody in the category picks up the lament and hands the viewer something to do next.

**The brand's own account runs the opposite way and misses from the other side.** Its trailing-quarter emotion tags run **Pride at 49.6% of spend and Hope at 38.8%, against Frustration at 19.5% and Anger at 2.6%**, and its awareness split is **Solution Aware 55.4% against Problem Aware 38.8%, with Unaware at $36.35 across the whole quarter**. `verified, live 2026-09-04`. Most of the money is on the outcome rather than the problem, and the account has essentially never tried to reach a woman who was not already unhappy with her coverage.

**So the open lane is the join, and nobody on either side is standing in it.** Mirror the problem the way the feed does, then be the only one in the category with a next step. That is `killer-performance-ads.md`'s story lead, which is how a stranger gets walked down the awareness ladder, applied to a category where the ladder's first rung is already crowded with people who never built a second one.

**Three pieces of evidence say the brand can do it, and one says how it goes wrong.**

The account's most-watched creative ever opens by agreeing with her: *"Health Insurance is a scam."* It holds **17.12% of viewers lifetime against 3.81% on the current top spender**. `verified` upstream. It works because it mirrors. Then it asks her to buy insurance, which is the natural conclusion's opposite, and `competitive-landscape.md` reads the same fact from the other side: the argument's real conclusion is the competitor with no ad account.

The one creative in this brand's history that earned agreement instead of argument is the phone-call skit. Roughly **12 clear recognitions in 1,342 comments**, under 1%, and almost all of them sit on that family. *"'It's out of network' 'But it's in the same building!' 'In a different hallway' whoever came up with this ad deserves a raise!!"* `verified` upstream. It holds **24.48%**, `verified, live 2026-09-04`, which is double the 12% floor `ad-account-analysis.md` sets and more than six times the current top spender.

And the failure mode is specific. Mirroring a **number** produces a bidding war, because she has a bigger number and she posts it: 82 of 1,342, 84% of them higher. Mirroring an **experience** produces recognition, because she has stood in that hallway and there is nothing to dispute. That is my cross-audit inference, layered on three upstream audits that each saw one half of it. Confidence: **mixed**, strong on the account evidence and thinner on the category read, which rests on nineteen videos.

**The counterweight, stated honestly.** The best-holding ad in this account produces a **$30.91 cost per lead**, which sits in the brand's worst band, `verified, live 2026-09-04`.
