## 2026-Q3 — quarterly cuts

- **`2026-Q3/90-day-creative-strategy-audit.md`** — the anchor. 5 June to 2 September 2026: $98,276.81, 136 ads, 4,336 leads, $22.67 cost per lead. **The account has become one ad** — `Moms43 - 4 - V3` took 43.5% of everything spent, the top three took 60.8%, the top ten took 88%.
- **`2026-Q3/90-day-performance-audit.md`** — spend fell 73.5% quarter over quarter ($374,508 to $99,267) while cost per lead held essentially flat ($22.65 to $22.64). A budget decision, not a performance failure. Carries a correction added 2026-09-04.
- **`2026-Q3/90-day-diversity-audit.md`** — the format and angle spread across the library. Carries a correction of 2026-09-04, verified by a lifetime pull, that overturns the doc's original claim about what the account has run.
- **`2026-Q3/quarterly-whitespace-analysis.md`** — **the largest under-invested proven buyer is the mom who already has insurance through her job and does not know she is allowed to leave it.** Two statics speak to her directly and both are dark. This is the evidence under roadmap Priority 2.
- **`2026-Q3/customer-review-audit.md`** — **Health For Moms has no customer reviews. Not few. None.** The record of the absence is the deliverable. Ran data-limited by design.
- **`2026-Q3/gaps-opportunities-inspo.md`** — where the lane is open. Written without a competitor watch list, so it starts by bounding what it is allowed to claim.
