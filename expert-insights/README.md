# Expert insights — Health For Moms

The brand's own curated expert intake. This is where operator and expert knowledge the team captures gets held, weighed, and turned into proposed changes to the brain — named tactics, current playbooks, the edge that keeps this brain ahead of a stock model.

**It lives at the repo root, not inside `parker-system/`, because it is brand property rather than method.** The read-only method mount never holds anything the team owns.

**Empty on 2026-09-04, at the end of the initial build, and that is expected.** Nothing is written here by the cold start. The folder is scaffolded so the surface exists and the paths in `CLAUDE.md` resolve; it fills the first time somebody runs `/expert-signal-intake`.

## The three folders

- **`inbox/`** — raw sources as handed over: an expert video, transcript, tweet, post, newsletter, podcast, article or report. Captured verbatim, nothing interpreted yet.
- **`curation/`** — the weighing. What the source actually claims, whether it holds against this brand's own evidence, and what it would change.
- **`context-update-candidates/`** — the exact, verbatim proposal of what would change in the brain, for a person to approve, modify or deny **before anything is written**. Nothing here is applied on its own.

## How to add something

Run **`/expert-signal-intake`** and hand it the source. It reads it, weighs it against this vault, and writes a proposal. The skill proposes; a person disposes.

## Why this matters for this brand specifically

`CLAUDE.md` names the single most common drift in a standing brain: the expertise gets read hard while the brain is being built and then quietly goes unused, and within a week the answers slide back to generic marketing. This folder and `brand-lens.md` are the two surfaces that fight it, and they only pay off when they get used.

Health For Moms has a particular reason to want this filled. Nothing in this brain says whether a recommended lane is empty because it is valuable or empty because somebody already tried it — no competitors are tracked, and the whole external audit branch is deferred. Operator knowledge about what actually works in health insurance lead generation is the cheapest available substitute for that missing competitive read.
