# Red against green

**concept_name:** Red against green

**idea:** Two numbers, side by side, in two colours, making the argument with no reading required. The category's biggest video writes every price on screen as it comes back down the phone line, red for what gets billed and green for the cash price, and never once says the word expensive. This brand's entire colour language is 41 state names in grey with green checkmarks. Nothing in its library uses colour to make a comparison, and the whole grievance in this category is a price.

**spark:** Two colours and two numbers is a static, not a shoot. This is the cheapest thing in the whole bank to make.

**hunt_lane:** Organic and format hunter, incidental capture during the Phase-3 backfill.

**source_read:** As each figure comes back over the phone, Hendry writes it into a notebook and mirrors it on screen. SVD, $2,760 in red against $2,208 in green. C-section, $3,103 in red against $2,482 in green. The two numbers are stacked so the gap is visible in a glance, and they stay on screen. She narrates the call around them but she never characterises the numbers. There is no adjective anywhere in the device. The colour does the whole job of saying which one you want.

**justification:** The absence inside the account is total and the proof is in the category. Nothing in this brand's library uses colour to make a comparison [`verified`, `sub-context-docs/visual-vocabulary.md` and `audits/2026-Q3/gaps-opportunities-inspo.md`]. The video that does is the largest in the mining library by a factor of nearly seven.

The reason to believe it transfers is that the account already proved the parent device. The "Approved State List ✔️" ad, `moms-53 3`, whose visual event is a wall of state names a viewer scans for her own, returned a **$16.01 cost per lead against a $22.10 account average** in the month to 2026-09-02, with the **highest click through rate of any ad in the account's top twenty at 3.15%** and a $0.67 cost per click [`verified`, `audits/2026-09/monthly-hook-audit.md`]. So a document in frame stops this audience cheaply. Colour is an upgrade to a device that is already working, not a new bet.

`parker-system/creative-strategy-context/static-ad-design.md` explains why it should carry: hierarchy decides whether the message is seen at all, and the money shot is the image that makes the argument with no reading required. Two numbers side by side in two colours is a money shot for a category whose whole grievance is a price. `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/hooks.md` names the shape as a Comparison hook, whose psychological basis is the contrast effect and value demonstration without an explicit claim, which matters enormously for a brand whose claims are the thing under legal pressure.

**Evidence picture.** One organic video, at very large scale, plus one in-account proof of the parent device. The link between them is `inferred`, not tested. And there is a real counterweight: `moms-53 3` buys the account's cheapest clicks and then converts them **worst among real spenders, at 5.97% leads per link click against `moms-63 2b`'s 12.25%** [`verified`, `strategy/strategic-roadmap.md`, which is **drafted and awaiting review, not approved**]. So a document buys the stop and does not automatically buy the form fill. Confidence: **mixed**.

**stage_of_awareness:** Solution aware. A comparison assumes she already accepts there is a decision to make. [`inferred`.]

**persona_or_audience_fit:** Runs wide. `already-knows` in particular, because `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/advertising-to-older-audiences.md` puts big, high contrast, one clear thing on screen at the top of what works for the 45 and over band, and the account's own state list device already reaches her.

**brand_fit:** The best compliance fit of any comparison idea here, and it needs care. The brand's non-negotiable rules ban naming a competitor and require savings to be phrased as "up to 30%" [`stated`, `sub-context-docs/brand-identity-analysis.md`]. A two colour comparison of two of the brand's own figures names nobody. A two colour comparison that puts a rival's price in red would break the rule outright. That distinction is the whole compliance question and it should be settled before anything is briefed.

**notes:** This is a design change rather than a production one. The brand already renders full frame text graphics with green checkmarks, so it can make this tomorrow [`inferred producible`, `sub-context-docs/visual-vocabulary.md`]. Pairs with `2026-09-04-the-faceless-bill-pan.md` and `2026-09-04-the-paycheck-that-came-back-empty.md`, which are the other two document ideas.
