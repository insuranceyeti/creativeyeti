# The correction economy

**concept_name:** The correction economy

**idea:** There is a standing volunteer army in this brand's comment sections whose entire job is correcting the ad's vocabulary. They tell the brand, and each other, that a deductible is not an out of pocket maximum. Several sign their corrections with a credential. A licensed broker. An insurance agent. A certified application counselor with thirty years in medical billing. The reusable idea is what that behavior proves about the category: **using the words right is itself a credential here**, and being publicly correct about the terms is a trust device no ad in this account has ever used.

**spark:** In this category, saying the word correctly is the credential. Nobody has ever spent three seconds of this account being right on purpose.

**hunt_lane:** Customer language, incidental capture during the Phase-3 backfill.

**source_read:** The ad's central line is "My deductible is $6,000. I have to spend $6,000 in medical expenses before everything is taken care of." Under it, the audience audits the sentence.

"That's not a deductible that's a medical maximum out of pocket. You should educate people, not misinform!!"

"As a licensed insurance broker this is VERY misleading in the beginning. The first half is talking about a medical maximum out of pocket. A deductible is something you pay before your insurance kicks in with co insurance."

"Deductible is different than maximum out of pocket 😃 -an insurance agent"

"A deductible ≠ out of pocket max. You should consider yourself lucky if your OOP Max is $6,000. A $6,000 deductible would the the amount you pay in full BEFORE copays kick in."

"Bc careful woth that Max Out of Pocket. U can have a $0 Deductible but an $8,000 Max Out of Pocket. Pay attention to the fine lines bc that's how they get u. Working with health insurance. This is kinda a sneak trick that's hidden out in the open."

And the sharpest one, which corrects the funnel rather than the vocabulary: "And they're holding pre-existing conditions against you, which is illegal. Their system automatically bounced me out. Since I'm a certified application counselor for the healthcare marketplace I know these things I've done medical billing for 30 years plus. I wouldn't waste my time on this page."

Notice what they are not doing. They are not asking a question and they are not buying. They are teaching, in public, unpaid, under an ad they think is wrong.

**justification:** The count is real and the spread is what makes it a pattern rather than a pile. **103 comments out of 1,342, across 13 distinct ad names**, are people correcting the deductible and out of pocket maximum distinction [`verified`, carried in `audits/2026-09/monthly-tiktok-mining.md` and `strategy/strategic-roadmap.md`, and the spread across 13 ads is what clears the denominator rule in `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/customer-review-mining-method.md`]. The vocabulary pass calls this brand's comment corpus its richest category for exactly this reason, and names the behavior "the correction economy" [`verified`, `personas/voice-of-customer/voc-category-jargon.md`].

The absence on the other side is complete. **Across 147 delivering ads, nobody credentialed has ever spoken** [`verified`, `audits/2026-Q3/gaps-opportunities-inspo.md`]. The account has put a clinician on screen exactly once. `MOMS30 - 1 - V20` opens on a pediatrician in a white lab coat checking a baby's heartbeat, ran **$7,407.34 lifetime for 381 leads at a $19.44 cost per lead on a 39.38% hook rate and a 2.44% hold rate**, and the whole transcript is one line at 0:11 from an unnamed narrator. The doctor never speaks [`verified live` 2026-09-04, carried in the gaps audit]. That is an unfinished test, not a failed one, and the 2.44% hold is what `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/killer-performance-ads.md` would predict from an opener that promises authority and a body that never delivers it.

**Evidence picture.** The count is strong for this corpus and the source is still singular. What is not known is whether a correction delivered by the brand converts or simply invites more corrections. Confidence: **strong** on the absence, **thin** on the outcome.

**stage_of_awareness:** Most aware for the correctors themselves, who mostly will not buy. Solution aware for the much larger group reading over their shoulder and learning the difference for the first time. The second group is the audience for the idea. [`inferred`.]

**persona_or_audience_fit:** `already-knows`, whose contextual identity in `personas/personas-profile.md` is literally the credentialed guard who steps in when a stranger asks for help. She is also the most attentive audience the account has, watching three to five times longer than the woman it courts [`verified`, from delivery data].

**brand_fit:** Good, and the brand already owns the asset. Its own rules require that a doctor character be a real licensed physician or that the language imply no false credential [`stated`, brand context section 8]. It routes every lead to real licensed partner advisors. So a credentialed speaker is compliant by construction if the casting is real. The gate is whether a partner advisor can be filmed and what compliance would let her say, which is a question only the brand can answer.
