# The real recorded call

## Idea

A genuinely unscripted recorded call with a real institution, filmed as it happens. It is the mechanic under the biggest video in the brand's whole category library. The brand's own version of this mechanic is comedy written in advance, and its best-holding creative in the account's history. This is the documentary version.

## Spark

The account already proved the *shape* of a phone call holds better than anything it makes — the version where the call is real is the same shape with the acting removed.

## Source read

Five minutes and thirty-two seconds, and the length is the proof. Dr Ashley Hendry, in glasses, a navy medical polo and a white lab coat, at her own desk in her own clinic. Neutral wall, a modern abstract painting, an examination table behind her, bright overhead office light, a practice logo for Mid-South Direct Primary Care in shot the whole time. Camera at eye level on a desk tripod, never moving. Phone on speaker, notebook open, pen in hand, already mid-call when the video starts:

> *"This is Dr. Ashley Hendry. I was calling to get a quote for a self-pay patient to see what the global vaginal delivery fee would be."*

The segment map is unusually simple, which is why it is adaptable. One, the credentialed introduction and the ask. Two, the first number spoken by the second party. Three, the follow-up that will not let the number stand alone: *"Does that cover the hospital facility fee or is that just the OBGYN group fee?"* Four, the correction that reframes everything: *"That is the normal price."* Five onward, the same three beats with a second hospital. **There is no monologue anywhere in it. Every sentence is either a question or an answer from someone else.**

**6,200,000 views, 661,800 likes, 208,000 shares, 22,700 comments, 108,770 saves** — 66.1% of all views in the 23-video library. Like rate 10.67%, share rate 3.36%, roughly double the library middle on both. `global_is_ad` is recorded as false, and a 3.36% share rate backs that up.

The brand's own version: `MOMS38 - 2 - V3` and `V4`, comedy written in advance, in an ad set the team itself named "20k skit v2." Hold rates 24.48% and 27.31%, the two highest in the account.

## Winning elements

- **format** — a real interaction the brand does not control, with a consenting second party.
- **hook** — an authority stated plainly in the first sentence and then demonstrated, using insider vocabulary ("global fee") without stopping to explain it.
- **proof** — a price handwritten in real time as a stranger reads it aloud, which is evidence rather than a claim.
- **pacing** — dead air, hold music and slow arithmetic. Cutting this to thirty seconds does not make a short version of it; it makes a different video, because the length is the proof.

## Justification

Biggest video in the library by a factor of seven, on the exact mechanic the account has already proved holds better than anything it makes. And the comment corpus keeps producing the confusion it answers: 103 comments across 13 ads are people correcting each other about the difference between a deductible and an out-of-pocket maximum — a woman quoted a number she does not understand and cannot verify. This video is ninety percent a woman verifying numbers out loud.

## Stage of awareness and audience fit

Problem aware, `inferred`. It sits under every persona because verification is universal here.

## Brand fit and risk

**Compliance-blocked as currently understood, and captured for exactly that reason.** Every filmed unit in this account carries a paid-actor disclaimer, and the capability the brand has never shown is filming a real interaction it does not control with a second party who consents. `visual-vocabulary.md` marks this **out of play** as a judgment, `inferred`, and routes it to the brand — because only the team knows whether a partner agent could be recorded doing real work.

The payload does not transfer either: self-pay pricing is not what this brand matches people to. The compliant substitute for the price comparison is the brand's own approved framing, "up to 30%" and "$0 deductible option," never a flat guarantee.

## Notes

The line that separates this from the brand's skit is small and total: a scripted denial is a joke about a system, and a recorded one is evidence about it. If the brand can film a licensed partner advisor making a real call on a family's behalf, this entry and `2026-09-04-the-credential-that-speaks.md` merge into one shoot.
