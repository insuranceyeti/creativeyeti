# Open your mouth in the first second

## Idea

The account's biggest ads spend their entire measured watch window playing music. The voice is the sound design in this category, and it should arrive immediately. This is the cheapest performance change available to the brand, it applies to the whole library at once, and no round of iteration has ever tried it.

## Spark

An account whose whole diagnosis is that it cannot hold anyone has been opening its biggest ads with ten to eighteen seconds of music.

## Source read

Four data points, none of them designed as a test [as of 2026-09-04]:

| Ad | First spoken word | Average play time |
|---|---|---|
| `moms-63 3e` | 0:11 | 4.03s |
| `moms-65 1a` | 0:10 | 3.41s |
| `Moms43 - 4 - V3` | **0:18** | 4.20s |
| `moms-53 3` — the one that opens talking | 0:00 | best deep retention in the account by a factor of nine |

On `Moms43 - 4 - V3`, the account's top spender: only **10.08% of its three-second viewers reach the quarter mark and 2.60% reach the halfway mark**. Read between those, well over ninety percent of the people who stop for this ad never hear a single word of what it is selling. They see a mother, a baby and a promise about $400, and they leave. On `moms-63 3e`, only **6.57% of three-second viewers reach fifteen seconds**, which means the risk-reversal line at 0:40 — the best line in the file — is delivered to about six percent of the people who stopped scrolling.

The silent clinician ad does the same thing with its one line at 0:11 against a four-second average play time.

The category feed runs the opposite way: **10 of the 19 Relevant videos have no background music at all**, and exactly one uses a music bed as a real choice.

## Winning elements

- **pacing** — the argument arriving inside the measured watch window rather than after it.
- **hook** — the strongest line in the file placed at second zero, which `iterations.md` calls the Cold Open.
- **format** — silence as a deliberate choice, which `hooks.md` counts as a sound hook rather than the absence of one.

## Justification

Read against the 80/20 rule in `killer-performance-ads.md`, where seconds one to three owe the viewer a reason to keep watching and seconds three to five owe her the best benefit or a re-hook, the account's biggest ad wins the thumb and then has nothing to say to the person it just stopped. Four files point the same way and not one was built as an experiment.

There is a ready-made test sitting unspent in the library: `moms-65 1e` already opens on the spoken line *"When did family health deductibles start looking like a down payment on a car?"* over different footage, and has taken **$7.24** of delivery. The asset exists and has never been funded.

The commercial point: if the silent runway is the cause, the fix costs nothing and applies to the entire library at once, which would be the cheapest performance gain available to this brand before November.

## Stage of awareness and audience fit

Problem aware, `inferred`. It is a pacing property rather than a message, so it sits under every persona.

## Brand fit and risk

No compliance surface. The risk is confounding: hook rate and hold rate are not the same lever, and the account's silent openers post strong hook rates — 45.84% on the flagship, 64.15% on the highest in the library. Cutting the runway may cost hook rate to buy hold. That trade is exactly what a test settles.

## Notes

The borrow is not "add music" or "remove music." It is that in this category the voice *is* the sound design, and it arrives immediately.
