# The Health For Moms idea bank

This is the icebox. It holds the half-baked ideas Parker has noticed for this brand, each one kept with the source it came from and a note on why it earned a place, so that nobody ever has to concept from a blank page.

It is not a strategy doc and it is not a brief. Nothing in here is a plan.

## What lives here

- `README.md` — this file.
- `index.md` — the searchable list of every active idea, plus the lane coverage read and the open loops this pass left behind.
- `entries/[YYYY-MM-DD]-[concept-slug].md` — one idea per file, in the schema below.

## The one rule that decides everything

**Ideation is not concepting.** This bank is the playground. The doors are open, the ideas are unfinished, and the job is to notice and log. Concepting is the separate, serious work of picking one idea out of here and building it into a brief with variations.

So an entry carries the source, what was liked about it, and the one-breath collision with this brand. It never carries the build. No worked-out storylines, no variations, no scripts, no briefs. Those happen later, in `/evaluate-ideas`, the sprint plan and brief creation. The full reasoning behind that split lives in `parker-system/creative-strategy-context/ideation-and-brainstorming.md`.

The second rule: **an entry has to be a fresh angle, not a mirror.** If an idea only tells the brand what it is already running, it hands a strategist nothing to test and it does not belong here. That rule bit hardest on this backfill, because a lot of what this brain knows is about the brand's own reputation and ad account.

## What every entry carries

`concept_name` · `idea` · `source_link` · `source_path` · `parker_media_links` · `source_type` · `source_name` · `date_added` · `winning_elements` · `spark` · `hunt_lane` · `source_read` · `justification` · `stage_of_awareness` · `persona_or_audience_fit` · `brand_fit` · `notes` · `status`.

Two of those do the most work and are easiest to get wrong.

**The spark** is the one-breath brand collision, recorded at the moment of noticing: what this is, and how it could carry Health For Moms. It has a gate, the articulation test. If the collision arrives in a second or two, write it down. If the fit has to be forced, leave the field empty and say so, because a laboured fit is evidence against the idea rather than for it. An entry with no spark is still a valid entry.

**The hunt lane** says which lens produced the entry — a persona lane, a named far-transfer category, the cold pass, the trend lane, or incidental capture during other work. It is what lets the evaluation see coverage and see what the bank is starving for.

`status` runs: raw idea → worth testing → adapted into concept → used → rejected → stale. Rejected entries stay when the reason teaches a brand boundary.

## Reading anything in here honestly

Four things are true of this brand and they shape every claim in every entry.

1. **This is lead generation with zero purchase events.** Every performance read is cost per lead and lead volume. ROAS, AOV, add-to-cart and purchase value are meaningless here and appear nowhere.
2. **The only first-party language surface is comments under the brand's own paid ads.** Customer reviews and post-purchase surveys are verified empty, Reddit is unreachable. So the honest sentence is always *commenters under the brand's paid ads said*, never *customers said*. Every persona claim caps at mixed confidence.
3. **No competitors are tracked.** One external brand exists in Parker for this account, Ethos, and it sells term life insurance, so it is an affinity reference and never a category read. Nothing in this bank is benchmarked against a rival.
4. **A winner has two gates and Parker can only see one.** Gate one is cost per lead. Gate two is lead quality, and it lives with the partner insurance agencies. Nothing in this bank is called a winner. Cheap means cheap on gate one.

Claim marks are used the same way throughout: `verified` means observed directly in a source or a pull, `stated` means a source says it and it has not been checked, `inferred` means reasoned from evidence with the basis named, `data-limited` means it cannot be resolved from what is available.

## How the bank grows

- **Continuously.** Any Parker run that happens to surface a genuinely reusable idea logs it. Most analytical passes surface nothing, and that is fine. Leave the capture empty rather than padding it.
- **Weekly**, through `/harvest-ideas`, which runs the structured hunt: a hunt brief, a cold pass before any feed opens, then the lanes — customer language, format and trend, the old-ads historian, and a far-transfer wildcard.
- **On demand**, when the team saves an idea or tells Parker to remember one.
- **Monthly curation**, which merges duplicates, marks stale ideas, promotes strong ones, and marks used ones when they become concepts, scripts or ads.

## What happens next

Capture and grading are two different jobs on purpose. This bank captures generously. `/evaluate-ideas` is the skeptical pass that grades the whole pile against the Phase-2 strategic roadmap and returns a ranked shortlist.

**One thing to know before that run.** `strategy/strategic-roadmap.md` carries `status: drafted, awaiting review` and `approved_by: null`. **It has not been approved by the brand.** Every reference to it in this bank says so. Grading a bank against an unapproved roadmap is a real thing to be careful about, because the ranking inherits whatever the roadmap got wrong, and nobody has signed off on it yet.
