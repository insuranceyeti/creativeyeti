# Idea bank — Health For Moms

This is Parker's living idea memory for this brand. It holds the half-baked ideas that get logged the moment they are noticed, so that nobody ever concepts from a blank page.

## What lives here

- `index.md` — the searchable list of active ideas, grouped by hunt lane, with source type, winning elements, awareness stage, status and compliance flag.
- `entries/[YYYY-MM-DD]-[concept-slug].md` — one idea per file, with its verbatim source, what was liked and why, the spark where one landed, and every Parker media link the source carried.

## What an entry is, and what it is not

An entry is an **idea**: a hook, a format, a storyline, a single line of customer language, or a visual, captured with a note on why it earned a place. Half-baked is the normal state.

An entry is **not a concept and not a brief**. Adapting an idea to this brand and building it into the two or three variations Meta needs is concepting, and it happens later in a dedicated session. No entry here contains a worked-out storyline, a set of variations, a script or a brief. The spark — the one-breath collision, "this format, pointed at this persona" — rides on top of the source and never replaces it.

Capture is a transfer, not a summary. The verbatim stays intact because the grading pass grades the real source, not a compression of it. A long entry is correct; a tidy summary is the failure.

## The rules this brand's bank runs under

- **Lead generation, zero purchase events.** ROAS, average order value, add-to-cart and purchase value are meaningless here and appear nowhere.
- **Gate one only.** Cost per lead is visible; lead quality lives with the partner insurance agencies. Nothing in this bank is called a winner.
- **Mixed confidence is the ceiling on every persona.** The only first-party language surface is comments under the brand's own paid ads. The honest sentence is always "commenters under the brand's paid ads said," never "customers said."
- **Compliance flags, not compliance deletions.** An idea that trips one of the brand's non-negotiable rules is still captured and flagged, so the grading pass can see what the rule costs. Two entries are `blocked` and ten are `conditional`.
- **No finished customer-facing copy.** Scripts, hooks and headlines route through their own craft skills with their own gate receipts.

## Freshness, not familiarity

The bar for this bank is a **new direction worth targeting**. It is not for the brand's own known assets reflected back. An award, a channel or a credibility marker the brand already advertises hands a strategist nothing to test. Before saving anything, the question is whether it gives a strategist a new direction or merely repeats what is already in the brand's ads.

## Grading happens elsewhere

This is the capture half of Phase 3. Ranking, shortlisting and killing all happen in `evaluate-ideas`, against the approved strategic roadmap. Do not rank here.
