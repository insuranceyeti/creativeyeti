# Idea evaluation — Health For Moms — 2026-09-04

## The call

**The four strongest ideas in this bank, in order, are the ones the account already proved and then switched off.** Not one of them is new. That is the finding, not a disappointment, and `persona-research-and-creative-strategy-process.md` is blunt about why: strong strategy usually looks like redeploying what already worked rather than inventing something new, and the first question about a proven message is how to redeploy it, not what to replace it with.

**1. `2026-09-04-you-can-decline-your-jobs-plan.md` — the employer switch.** It leads because it is the only idea in the bank that four separate strategy reads converged on without seeing each other, and because the account has already bought its evidence. Two statics carrying this message returned 3,815 leads at $11.79 across $44,972.40, turning 19.04% of their link clicks into leads against video's 8.28% [as of `strategic-roadmap.md`, 2026-09-04]. Both are dark.

**2. `2026-09-04-restart-the-static-modality.md` — the format that carries it.** It ranks second rather than first only because it is a container and the switch is the cargo. The account has run 529 static ads for $168,907.73 and 13,735 leads at $12.30 apiece, and has spent nothing on one in 180 days. A static's click becomes a lead at twice a video's rate on denominators of 82,871 and 312,052 link clicks.

**3. `2026-09-04-the-woman-who-already-closes-them.md` — move her to frame zero.** The highest-confidence, fastest-to-ship item in the whole bank. The woman is already cast, already filmed, already carrying the two biggest ads in the account. What changes is the edit order. Four seconds of difference separates 3.81% hold from 17.12% on two ads within $3,300 of each other in spend.

**4. `2026-09-04-one-specific-moment-per-headline.md` — the sentences that fill the static restart.** The two cheapest leads in a 529-ad library, at $6.99 and $9.62, are both one sentence about one moment happening to one person. This is what number two runs on once the claim is repaired.

**Before any of those ship, the roadmap's Priority 1 has to land, and the bank does not contain it.** Two claims run on 127 of the account's 128 ad-name groups, carrying $100,066.79 of the window's $100,067.42, and they travel together in one shared block of body copy [as of `strategic-roadmap.md`, 2026-09-04]. That is one edit, not a hundred, and it is not a creative idea, so it is right that no entry redeploys it. What matters for this rank is the inheritance: every new asset built from the four ideas above will pick that block up unless it is fixed first. Rank one through four are gated on that edit, not on any research.

**Two things about how this was graded, said once.**

First, **every band clears gate one only.** Lead quality is unmeasured. The account fired 44 `Call` events across 16 ads against 4,421 leads in the trailing 90 days, and the pixel built to track a booked consultation returns no data at all. So no idea here is ranked on an assumption about who enrolls, no cost per lead below is called good for the business, and nothing in this document is called a winner.

Second, **format labels were not allowed to earn a rank.** This build found repeatedly that the tags do not predict behaviour: two ads both tagged Skit sent 40.9% and 12.4% of their money to women 45 and over, and the one that reached her is a woman confessing her own situation to camera while the one that did not is a comedy sketch [as of `persona-strategy-input.md`, 2026-09-04]. Every band below is set on the specific creative mechanism described in the entry, never on the label attached to it. One entry, the satirical skit batch, is graded down explicitly for resting on the label.

**What the rank sits on.** Sixteen entries were held at LOW because the evidence under them is real but thin, which is the honest state of a bank built in one backfill day. Three were cut outright. Three were folded into stronger versions of themselves. Two are held behind a question only the brand can answer. The full accounting is in the cuts section.

**And the whole order is provisional.** The roadmap it grades through is `drafted, awaiting review`. A rank against an unapproved direction is still useful, because it turns a pile into a queue, but it is a queue to hold lightly. If the direction is approved with shifted priorities, re-synthesize from the top rather than patching this order.

## Ranked shortlist, by priority

### Priority 1 — Fix the two claims

The roadmap's first priority is a repair rather than a concept, so the bank cannot serve it directly. What the bank does hold is the strategic half of the same move, and the roadmap names it plainly: when regulators publicly define what bad looks like, a brand that is visibly and checkably not that has something real to stand on. Every credibility marker this brand carries today is its own assertion. Four entries work that seam. All four are cheap, all four are compliant as captured, and all four get better the moment the copy block is fixed.

*The priority and the persona.* Priority 1, the credibility half. It sits on all three personas at one moment: the beat between seeing the ad and handing over a phone number.

*The lever.* Messaging first, then proof and format second.

*Evidence band: MEDIUM, and the confidence is unusually good for a MEDIUM.* Four independent surfaces were checked and all four came back empty on 2026-09-04: zero customer reviews, zero press, no Better Business Bureau profile for the operating entity, and the FBI's discount medical insurance scam alert plus the FTC's scam page ranking ahead of the brand's own site [as of `reputation-analysis.md` and `gaps-opportunities-inspo.md`, 2026-09-04]. Three commenters under the brand's paid ads ran that exact search in public and reported the result: *"A Google search doesn't pull anything about this. Is it legit?"* (2025-07-24), *"How come when I search for this on Google, nothing comes up?"* (2025-08-07), and one who concluded from the blank alone, *"sounds like a scam to me."* The word "scam" appears in 48 of 1,342 comments across 15 distinct ad names, first 2025-01-13 and most recently 2026-09-03. What keeps this at MEDIUM rather than HIGH is that nothing in the account has ever tested it: zero ads are built to answer the search, so the invisibility is verified and the fix is not.

*Production tier and the verdict.* Tier 1 to Tier 3 depending on how it is built, and the messaging input already set the shape: this is a proof layer inside the ad, not the promise at the top, because leading with a rebuttal announces a doubt to viewers who had not formed one.
