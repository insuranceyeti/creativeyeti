## How to read this index

- **Status** is `raw idea` on all 45. Nothing in this bank has been adapted into a concept, used, rejected or gone stale yet, because the bank is one day old.
- **Compliance** is the extra column this brand needs. `none` means the idea trips no hard rule. `conditional` means part of it needs a brand or counsel decision before briefing. `blocked` means the idea as stated trips a hard rule and is captured anyway so evaluation can see what the rule costs.
- **Lane** is what makes a per-lane view possible later, and it is what tells the evaluation pass which lanes this bank is starving in.

## What the evaluation pass should know before it grades

**Twelve entries are flagged for compliance and captured anyway.** Two are `blocked` as written — the January subsidy cliff and the real recorded call — and ten more are `conditional`, meaning a piece of them waits on a brand or counsel answer. None was dropped. The rule this bank follows is that an idea that trips a hard rule is still captured, flagged, so grading can see what the rule costs rather than never learning it existed.

**Four questions gate large parts of this bank and only the brand can answer them.** They are already in the roll-up as brand-routed items and they are repeated here because several entries are unbriefable until they come back: why the statics stopped around March; whether a licensed partner advisor can be filmed and what compliance would let her say; what the "up to 30%" savings claim compares against; and where the real boundary sits on naming government programmes.

**Gate two is dark on all 45.** The brand's own winner definition is cost per lead first, then lead quality, and lead quality lives with the partner insurance agencies. Every efficiency figure in this bank clears gate one only. If the agencies come back and say the cheap leads enrol worse than the expensive ones, the grading below this bank can invert, and it should.

**The lane coverage is uneven and that is a finding, not an accident.** Account archaeology is the fattest lane at 18 entries, because this account has proved and abandoned an unusual amount. Customer language is thin at 5, and the reason is structural: the corpus is one source type, 60.1% of it sits on a single creative family, and 64.0% falls in two months. Until one independent read of how mothers in this category talk exists, that lane cannot get much fatter honestly. The cold pass and the far-transfer wildcard were not run at all.

**Three entries carry no Parker media links** and each says so in its own words: the "no window" entry, which rests on a keyword absence across 2,122 ads; the December reset, which rests on comment verbatims where `permalink_url` is null on all 1,342 rows; and the verification moment, which rests on live web searches recorded in `reputation-analysis.md` that carry no Parker handle.

## Maintenance

Capture is continuous. Add an entry whenever a source contains a reusable creative pattern, whenever the team saves or names an idea, or whenever a repeated pattern shows up across brand docs. Curate monthly: merge duplicates, mark stale ideas, promote strong ones to `worth testing`, and mark used ideas when they become concepts, scripts, briefs or ads. Preserve rejected ideas when the reason teaches a brand boundary — this brand's rejection of synthetic presenters is exactly that kind of lesson and is carried inside the casting entries rather than deleted.
