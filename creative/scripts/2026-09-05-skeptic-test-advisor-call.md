## Why this script

The single most-liked comment under your paid ads, 47 likes, opens with "This sounds too good to be true." That is the skeptic test's opening line, said by a commenter under your own ad, and it drew more agreement than any other reaction in 1,322 records. Your audience is insured, angry about her deductible, and doesn't believe you. So this ad lets her be the skeptic out loud, and puts her doubt in front of the one person in your funnel who can actually answer it: a real licensed partner advisor.

Across 147 delivering ads, nobody credentialed has ever spoken. You've put a doctor character on screen in live action, once silent (`MOMS30 - 1 - V20`) and once as a comedy sketch (`MOMS39 - 5 - V5`), and stitched a real medical creator once (`Moms44 - 1 - V3`), but you've never let your own advisor open her mouth. The bet is that a calm, plain answer from a real advisor does what a savings number can't: it makes the claim checkable.

**Casting the advisor, and why it matters twice.** Cast a licensed advisor in her late forties. The roadmap and the creator-talent read both land on the same person: an advisor in her forties or fifties satisfies the credential need (Priority 2's proof layer) and the older-woman casting need (Priority 3) in one shoot. Your number one stated creative bottleneck is casting. This is one hire that serves two priorities.

**What this is built on, honestly.** The phone-call grammar is your best-holding shot, 27.31% and 24.48% on `MOMS38 - 2 - V4` and `V3`, though not your only one over the 12% floor. The skeptic arc comes from `moms-63 3e`, but be clear about what that ad proves: its 42.87% hook rate and $15.46 cost per lead belong to the silent POV opener, which this script drops. Its body arc, the confession and the worst-case close, played to about 6.57% of the people who stopped, at a 2.91% hold. So this script is the **first real test of the skeptic arc inside a hook built to carry it**, not a proven arc re-used. That is the honest framing and it is still the right swing.
